Corporate Overview

Evolution Optimization Solutions (EOS) is a privately held biotechnology company focused on engineered survivability & biological augmentation for extreme environments. Founded in 1996, EOS partners with national laboratories, defense agencies, and private organizations to create applied biotechnology solutions.

"Creating the biological edge — responsibly, efficiently, and with precision."

Highlights — 1996–1999

  • 1996 — Company founded; initial seed funding secured from private venture consortium.
  • 1997 — First isolated program: Cold-Resistance Protein Stabilization (CRPS).
  • 1998 — Partnership agreement with national aerospace program for long-term habitation studies.
  • 1999 — New developments lead to increased funding from DARPA and private sector partners.

Featured Research: Spike Stability Mapping

Our SP-NODE infrastructure enables high-resolution molecular exploration of viral surface proteins, protein folding dynamics, and candidate neutralizing structures.

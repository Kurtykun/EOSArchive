## THE INFECTED

*“They were meant to outlive us. That was the point.”*
<br>
<br>
*"They're not corpses. They're the perfected end state of a once human body refined and reduced for a singular purpose. Survival at any cost through any means."*
<br>
<br>
*"Gaunt, hairless flesh pulled taut. Eyes open, unblinking, eerily observant watching always watching. Their lips recede, muscles engorged and the jaw locked open just enough to expose a permanent crescent of pure bone white teeth. A mocking grin, though there is no expression, there is no emotion inside this creature. -And make no mistake, a creature is exactly what they are, they aren't your loved one any more. Any hope, dream or moment of happiness in their memory is gone."*
<br>
<br>
*"Their hearts still beat slowly pushed by esoteric bioelectric rhythms that persist beyond their cortical death. Their blood is thick and dark yet completely devoid of any pathogen or sickness. They do not carry the virus. They are the virus. Every cell is integrated, rewritten and repurposed to form this new whole. They do not rot. They do not sleep. They do not stop."*
<br>
<br>
*"The perfect organism, able to gain energy from any biological source; plant matter, decaying waste, meat. Do they simply follow movement and sound or are we being hunted? Can they see our heat, smell our blood sense our very being? Perhaps they are drawn to memory, to familiar spaces, to the living simply because we are what they were meant to leave behind."*
<br>
<br>
*"They feel no pain. They suffer not. Their cool bodies are resistant to temperature, infection, radiation and even time itself. The Tithonus virus worked exactly as it was meant to. It created a body that can endure hostile environments for generations. All it cost was removing everything that made us human; emotion, cognition and identity. A self sustaining fortress. They remain."*
<br>
<br>
**"We won't be the survivors of the fall of humankind. They will."**

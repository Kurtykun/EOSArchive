Diversifying for Tomorrow

EOS announces renewed strategic partnerships with government agencies, international research institutions and selected private sector partner to accelerate breakthrough biotechnology development by bringing new investment into the field.

Founder Dr. Peter Hughes Departs for House of Lords

Corktown, MA December 15, 2008 - Evolution Optimization Solutions announces that founder and Chief Executive Officer Dr. Peter Hughes will be returning to the UK and taking up a seat in the house of lords.

"It has been an extraordinary journey building this company from a small research startup with a small loan of a million dollars from my father. Into this company at the forefront of biotechnology innovation. I am sorry to be leaving but excited at the prospect of developing scientific policy at the highest levels in Westminster, I am confident that EOS is well-positioned for continued success and I look forward to meeting my sucessor when one is found."

— Dr. Peter Hughes, Founder & Outgoing CEO

Dr. Hughes will take his seat in the House of Lords in January 2009 and will be advising the UK government on biotechnology policy. He will maintain a consulting role with EOS through the transition period.

Dr. Jean Lira Named Research Director

In a significant restructuring move, Dr. Jean Lira has been promoted to Research Director, placing her at the forefront of EOS's strategic pivot toward protective biotechnology.

"We stand at a critical juncture in biotechnology. The partnerships we've formed provide unprecedented resources, but with that comes responsibility and risk. EOS must lead not just in innovation, but in ensuring our research serves to protect rather than potentially harm."

— Dr. Jean Lira, Research Director

Dr. Lira brings over 15 years of experience in virology and has been the driving force behind EOS's safety protocols and ethical research standards. In her new role she will oversee the company's transition toward biological countermeasure research.
